Real Estate Market Analytics Project — From Raw Data to Actionable Insights!

I’m excited to share one of my most recent data analytics projects where I took on a real estate dataset, cleaned it from scratch, performed in-depth analysis, and visualized key business insights using Power Query, Python, and Power BI.

📝 Project Overview

The primary objective was to analyze real estate sales transactions from six major Nigerian cities to understand the market dynamics influencing:

✅ Sales Price Distribution

✅ Profitability Trends

✅ Impact of Seller Type

✅ Effect of Furnishing Status

✅ Property Type Preferences


The focus was not just on creating beautiful visualizations but on generating practical, data-driven insights that could inform property investors, agents, and policymakers.

🔨 Workflow Summary: From Raw Data to Insight

Step 1: Data Cleaning with Power Query

Imported raw data containing inconsistencies, duplicates, and missing values.

Cleaned and standardized key columns (state, property type, seller type, furnishing status).

Handled missing values, corrected typos, and ensured consistency in categorical fields.


Step 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with Python

Leveraged Pandas and Matplotlib/Seaborn to perform:

Outlier detection

Distribution analysis

Correlation checks between sale prices and property features

Validation of summary statistics before visualization.


Step 3: Interactive Dashboard with Power BI

Created a fully interactive Power BI dashboard featuring:

KPIs: Total Sales, Profit/Loss, State/Property Type counts

Bar charts comparing average sale price by state and seller type

Donut charts for furnishing status and state-wise distribution

Line/Area charts showcasing price trends across property types

Slicers and filters allowing dynamic exploration by location, property, and furnishing status.

📊 Key Insights Extracted

✅ Total Sale Price Volume: ₦135 Million
✅ Total Profit/Loss Recorded: ₦16 Million

✅ State Analysis:

Lagos stands out with the highest average sale price (₦289.6K), accounting for 17.9% of total sales.

Ibadan shows the lowest average sale price (₦253.8K).


✅ Seller Type Trends:

Properties sold by individual sellers fetched higher average prices compared to realtors across most states—indicating a potential margin opportunity in direct sales.


✅ Furnishing Status Impact:

Furnished properties commanded the highest average prices (₦279.9K).

Unfurnished properties trailed at ₦252.0K, highlighting the value addition from interior investments.

✅ Property Type Insights:

Houses and Townhouses recorded the highest average sale prices (~₦272K).

Apartments were the most affordable category (₦267K on average).

Clear demand preference for larger family dwellings over compact units.
